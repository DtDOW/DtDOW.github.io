## Organising my first event with juniors. 

So last night, Manas and I organised a game night with the 1st Year students. It was a fun experience and a great excuse to interact with eachother. I hope the juniors enjoyed it too. We played three matches in Battle Royale, and the seniors won, of course (the juniors completely obliterated us in the first two, but we pulled ourselves together in the third one because we didn’t want to spend money  T-T).

## Future Events

We are thinking of organising more such events in the future, preferably a BGMI match this Friday. Hopefully, we will get a positive response, and if we continue to receive such feedback, we will take more such initiatives. We want to create a fun environment where everyone enjoys and learns. Personally, I love games so much! I am currently working on game development to create my own games. Hopefully, in the near future, I can play and enjoy those games with everyone. :)
