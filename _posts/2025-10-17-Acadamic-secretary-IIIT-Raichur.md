## Academic Secretary of IIIT Raichur

Hello everyone!

I’m proud and incredibly excited to share that I’ve been elected as the Academic Secretary of IIIT Raichur.

This is not just a title, it’s a responsibility and an opportunity to contribute to the academic growth of our institute and fellow students.

Our work has just begun! From bringing various facilities to organizing academic events, it’s going to be an exciting journey!! I will be looking forward to working with all of you, ensuring to develop an academic culture that not only focuses on grades but rather the curiosity of students to learn and understand new things!!
